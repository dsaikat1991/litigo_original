# Changelog

What's new in Litigo, most recent first.

## Litigo Pro and email reminders

- Introduced **Litigo Pro** (₹299/month or ₹2,999/year) — unlimited active cases. The free plan covers up to 5 active cases and is otherwise fully featured.
- Added **email reminders** for upcoming hearings and tasks — turn them on per-threshold in Settings, alongside the existing in-app reminders.
- Search got faster and smarter — live results as you type, plus filters for date range and tags.
- Redesigned the homepage and added a real Pricing page.

## Case Timeline and richer case records

- Added a **Timeline** tab on every case — hearings, completed tasks, and notes merged into one chronological view of what happened, in order.
- You can now **link related cases** — an interim application, appeal, or execution to its parent case.
- Tasks can be flagged as **critical** (e.g. a limitation date) so they stand out wherever they appear.
- Upload **documents** to a case — petitions, orders, evidence, scanned filings — kept private and secure.
- Added a **Today** page — a printable list of everything on your court calendar today.
- Hearing records can now capture bench, judge, courtroom, stage, and attendance, for when you need that level of detail.

## Building out the core diary

- Sign in with **Google**.
- Case pages reorganized into tabs: Hearings, Tasks, Research, Documents, and Notes & Learnings.
- Added a **Research** tab for tracking case law, statutes, and articles per case.
- Added a notification bell so upcoming reminders are visible from anywhere in the app.
- Added standalone **Notes** for learnings that aren't tied to any one case.
- Added a **Calendar** view of every upcoming hearing.
- Added global **search** across cases, hearings, and notes.
- Profile photos, password reset, and a public landing page.

## Launch

- Litigo launched — track cases, hearings, and notes in one digital diary, built for solo advocates in India.
